About

I am an applied mathematician at CEREMADE, Université Paris Dauphine-PSL, where I defend my PhD on 17 November 2026 under the supervision of Emmanuel Lepinette. From December 2026 I am an ATER (temporary lecturer and researcher) at Paris Dauphine-PSL.

My research is in applied probability and mathematical modelling for risk management under market frictions and model uncertainty. My thesis gives explicit super-hedging prices and strategies for markets with proportional transaction costs; the model needs no probability law for prices, only the range of their moves, and the resulting algorithm is checked against exact benchmarks, tested on S&P 500 data and released as open-source software.

Before my PhD, I taught algebra full-time for four years as professeure agrégée at the Preparatory Institute for Engineering Studies of Monastir, after ranking 3rd in the agrégation. I hold a Master in Mathematics and Applications from the Université de Monastir, where I ranked 1st, with a thesis on Gaussian processes at LIMOS, Université Clermont Auvergne.

Research

I combine stochastic analysis, convex duality and dynamic programming with numerical methods, open-source software and validation on real data.

Explicit hedging under costsRecursive closed-form super-hedging prices and the interval of all optimal hedges in discrete time, calibrated and tested on market data.Papers [1], [3]
Continuous timeA dynamic programming principle in Kabanov's solvency-cone model, and the path towards a path-dependent HJB–QVI.Paper [6]
Random sets and ordered structuresConditional random sets in Polish spaces; concentration inequalities in Riesz spaces.Papers [2], [4], [7]

Broader interests: stochastic control, Monte Carlo methods, uncertainty quantification and probabilistic machine learning.
